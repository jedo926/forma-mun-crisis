RULES & RESOURCES

Procedure, delegate guides and approved documents.

General crisis-committee flow for orientation. The chairs' official rules of procedure always take precedence.
  1. Roll call. Delegates answer "present" or "present and voting".
  2. Crisis briefing. The chairs open with the scenario and the first update.
  3. Moderated caucus. Timed speeches on a topic set by the motion.
  4. Unmoderated caucus. Free movement to negotiate and draft.
  5. Directives. Written actions submitted to the chairs and voted on by committee.
  6. Crisis notes. Private actions sent to the crisis backroom on behalf of your character.
  7. Crisis updates. The chairs announce how the world responds to your actions.

POINTS

  • Point of order — procedure was not followed.
  • Point of personal privilege — comfort or audibility.
  • Point of parliamentary inquiry — a question about procedure.
  • Give it a clear title and list your sponsors and signatories.
  • State concrete actions: who does what, with which resources, by when.
  • Stay inside your character's powers and the committee's scope.
  • Keep it short. Chairs act on what is written, not what was meant.
  • Address the note to the backroom and sign it with your character.
  • Explain the action, the method, and what you want to achieve.
  • Build arcs over several notes rather than one giant move.
  • Speak in character, respect other delegates and the chairs.
  • Technology use follows the chairs' instructions on the day.
  • Ask the chairs whenever something is unclear.
